About EcoTeam

Welcome to EcoTrack! We are a dedicated group of individuals passionate about environmental sustainability, community action, and ecological awareness.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower communities and individuals with the tools, knowledge, and resources needed to reduce their environmental footprint and live more sustainable lives.

What We Do

  • Provide up-to-date environmental news and insights.
  • Offer creative guides and help for handicrafts made from recycled materials.
  • Map out eco-friendly locations, recycling centers, and green spaces.
