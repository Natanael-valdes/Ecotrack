💡 Quick Eco-Tips for Crafting

🧼

Clean Waste First

Always wash bottles, cans, and containers thoroughly before reuse to avoid bad odors or mold.

✂️

Safety First

Use child-safe scissors for paper and get adult assistance when cutting thick plastic or metal edges.

🎨

Eco-Friendly Paints

Use water-based acrylic paints instead of solvent-based sprays to reduce harmful fumes.
